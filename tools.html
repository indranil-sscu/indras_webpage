Energy, frequency & wavelength

Photon / per-particle quantities: E = hν = hc/λ = hc·ν̃; molar values multiply by NA; temperature is E/kB. 1 Hartree = 27.211386 eV = 219474.63 cm⁻¹ = 627.5095 kcal/mol.
