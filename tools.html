Spectral Jacobian: wavelength ⇄ wavenumber

A spectrum is a density per unit of its axis, so changing axes needs the Jacobian |dλ/dν̃| = λ²/10⁷: I(ν̃) = I(λ)·λ²/10⁷ (λ in nm, ν̃ in cm⁻¹), and conversely I(λ) = I(ν̃)·ν̃²/10⁷. This keeps the integrated intensity unchanged; simply relabelling the x-axis distorts line shapes and band ratios. Header and comment lines are ignored; the data never leave your browser.
