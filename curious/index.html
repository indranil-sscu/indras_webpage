How these work

Every experiment runs a real simulation or calculation in your browser, and puts the result next to what theory predicts. In the probability experiments, the more you run, the closer the two get: the law of large numbers. In the pendulum and fractal explorers the computer solves the equations step by step, and the page shows how far that numerical answer can be trusted.

Ideas for new experiments are welcome on GitHub.
