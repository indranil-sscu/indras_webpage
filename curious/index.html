How these work

Every experiment runs a real simulation or calculation in your browser, and puts the result next to what theory predicts. The more you run, the closer the two get. That idea, the law of large numbers, connects all three.

More experiments are being built: pendulums, wave interference and fractals. Ideas are welcome on GitHub.
