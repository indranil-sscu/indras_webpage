## Indranil Roy

I am a PhD candidate in the **Solid State and Structural Chemistry Unit (SSCU), Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru**, working with Prof. Vivek Tiwari in the [Multidimensional Spectroscopy and Imaging Group](https://sites.google.com/view/musig/).

I study how vibrational and electronic motions couple on femtosecond timescales, and how this vibronic mixing moves energy and charge through molecular aggregates, photosynthetic light-harvesting systems, singlet-fission materials and strongly coupled light–matter (polariton) states. My work combines coherent two-dimensional electronic spectroscopy with vibronic model Hamiltonians.

### Research interests

- Ultrafast and multidimensional (2D) electronic spectroscopy
- Vibronic coupling and exciton dynamics in molecular aggregates
- Singlet exciton fission
- Strong light–matter coupling and molecular polaritons
- Nonlinear response theory and double-sided Feynman diagrams

## About this site

Indra's Webpage is separate from my academic homepage: it is where I build tools I need in the lab, and experiments that I hope make mathematics and physics more approachable.

- **Everything runs in your browser.** There is no server and no tracking; your data and images never leave your device.
- **The science is checked.** Each tool shows its equations, units and assumptions, and the calculations are covered by automated tests against analytical results and published benchmarks.
- **It is open source.** The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/indranil-sscu/indras_webpage) (plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on GitHub Pages). If you find a mistake or have a suggestion, please [open an issue](https://github.com/indranil-sscu/indras_webpage/issues/new).
