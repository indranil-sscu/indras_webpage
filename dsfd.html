Enumerate every Liouville pathway contributing to an n-th-order nonlinear signal in a chosen phase-matching direction, and draw its double-sided Feynman diagram. Each diagram shows its sign (−1)n, classification (GSB / SE / ESA / DQC), and the corresponding term of the response function. ← All tools

1 · Signal

2 · Level scheme

Examples: two-level system e / empty; three-level ladder e / f; excitonic dimer a, b / f.

3 · Show

Conventions (Mukamel; Hamm & Zanni). Time runs upward; the left line is the ket, the right line the bra; the system starts in |g⟩⟨g|. An arrow pointing right carries +k, pointing left −k; an arrow pointing toward the diagram excites, away from it de-excites. The final dashed arrow is the emitted signal field, which acts on the ket and leaves a population. Each diagram has sign (−1)n, n = number of bra interactions, and contributes ∏μ · G(tn)…G(t1) with Gab(t) = exp(−iωabt − Γabt) in the impulsive limit. Rotating-wave approximation: only resonant (up/down by one manifold) interactions are kept.
