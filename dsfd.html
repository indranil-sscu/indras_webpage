Enumerate every Liouville pathway that contributes to an n-th-order nonlinear signal in a chosen phase-matching direction, and draw its double-sided Feynman diagram. Each diagram is classified (GSB / SE / ESA / DQC, or SFG / DFG at second order) and shown with its sign, wave-vector sum, time-evolution phase and response-function term. Theory, conventions & references
S(n)(tn, …, t₁) = (i/ħ)n ⟨μ(tn + … + t₁) [μ(tn−1 + … + t₁), … [μ(t₁), [μ(0), ρ(−∞)]] … ]⟩ = (i/ħ)n Σpathways (−1)nbra Rα
ksig = Σi ±ki,   Gab(t) = exp(−iωabt − Γabt),   e.g. rephasing SE: exp(+iωegt₁ − iωegt₃)
ket / bra
left and right rails, acting on |·⟩ and ⟨·| of ρ; time runs upward from ρ = |g⟩⟨g|
arrow direction
toward a rail: absorption (that side is excited); away: emission (de-excitation). Right-pointing arrows carry +k, left-pointing −k
signal
dashed arrow: the emitted field, acting last on the ket and returning ρ to a population
sign
(−1)nbra, nbra = number of bra-side interactions before the signal
rephasing
the t₁ and t₃ coherences oscillate with opposite signs of frequency, so inhomogeneous dephasing is undone (photon echo)
GSB, SE, ESA
ground-state bleach (|g⟩⟨g| during t₂), stimulated emission (excited-state population or coherence, signal from e → g), excited-state absorption (third interaction excites to f)

Why ESA is negative. ESA pathways always contain an odd number of bra-side interactions, so the rule (−1)nbra already gives them the opposite sign to GSB and SE. An extra factor of (−1) for ESA would double-count it. The rotating-wave approximation keeps only resonant interactions (one manifold up or down per field), and the impulsive limit gives each interval its own propagator Gab(ti). For pump–probe both pump interactions are degenerate (t₁ = 0), so rephasing and non-rephasing pathways both radiate along kpr.
